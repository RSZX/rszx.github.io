Privacy Policy.

RailDrop is a game developed by Triquinete (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is collected when you play RailDrop on Android, and the choices you have.

01 / OVERVIEW

Summary

  • We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address or any account.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • RailDrop shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent first.
  • The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
02 / LOCAL STORAGE

Information stored on your device

RailDrop saves your settings (sound, haptics, night mode), scores, level progress and chosen ball finish in a file on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

03 / GOOGLE ADMOB

Advertising (Google AdMob)

RailDrop shows two kinds of ads:

  • full-screen ads at natural breaks, such as after a game ends;
  • optional reward videos, which you choose to watch (for example, to continue a level).

To provide these ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and clicks;
  • diagnostic data.

Depending on your consent and location, this information may be used to show personalised ads, or only non-personalised (limited) ads.

Google’s handling of this data is described in:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
04 / GDPR & UK GDPR

Your consent choices (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, RailDrop shows a consent message (Google’s User Messaging Platform) the first time you play. You can:

  • accept personalised advertising;
  • reject it, in which case you may still see non-personalised (limited) ads;
  • manage your choices by purpose and partner.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy inside the game. The game works the same whatever you choose. Declining never limits access to any part of the game.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).

05 / US PRIVACY

US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with consumer privacy laws (such as California), you can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising through the privacy message shown in the app, or in Settings → Privacy.

06 / GOOGLE PLAY BILLING

In-app purchase (“Remove Ads”)

The optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account, so your purchase can be restored on new devices. We never receive your card or payment details.

07 / AGE & PRIVACY

Children

RailDrop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

08 / THIRD PARTIES

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except as described above (Google AdMob for ads, Google Play for purchases).

09 / DATA CONTROL

Data retention and deletion

  • We hold no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • To delete your local game data, uninstall RailDrop or clear its data in Android settings.
  • For data held by Google, use your Google Account controls: myaccount.google.com
10 / USER RIGHTS

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es ). Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests concern Google’s services and are best made through Google. You can always contact us with questions.

11 / UPDATES

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
