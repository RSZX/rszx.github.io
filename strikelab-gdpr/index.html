Privacy Policy.

StrikeLab is a bowling analysis app developed by Triquinete (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is used when you use StrikeLab on Android, and the choices you have.

01 / OVERVIEW

Summary

  • We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address or any account.
  • Your videos are analyzed on your phone. Videos, shots, scores and settings stay on your device and are never sent to us.
  • StrikeLab has no ads and no analytics of its own.
  • The optional “StrikeLab Full” purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The on-device text recognition library from Google may send Google basic diagnostic information (see section 04).
02 / LOCAL STORAGE

Information stored on your device

StrikeLab saves the shot videos you record or import, the analysis of each shot (ball path, body position, metrics and advice), your games and scores, your balls, lane setups and settings in the app’s private storage on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

03 / PERMISSIONS

Camera, photos and videos

  • Camera is used to record your shots (including live mode) and to photograph the lane’s score screen so the scores can be read. Pictures and video are processed on your device only.
  • Photos and videos you pick from your gallery are opened only when you choose them, through Android’s photo picker. StrikeLab cannot see the rest of your gallery.
  • Highlight videos you choose to save are written to your gallery (Movies/StrikeLab).
  • Notifications show the progress of an analysis running in the background, and when it is done.
04 / GOOGLE LIBRARIES

Google ML Kit

StrikeLab reads scores from score screen photos with Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs entirely on your device: your photos are not sent to Google or to us. ML Kit may send Google basic diagnostic and usage information (such as device model, Android version, app version and how well the feature performs) to keep the library working and improve it. Google’s handling of this data is described in the ML Kit Terms and the Google Privacy Policy .

Pose estimation and ball detection use Google MediaPipe and OpenCV models that run on your device and send nothing.

05 / GOOGLE PLAY BILLING

In-app purchase (“StrikeLab Full”)

The free version includes a limited number of shot analyses. The optional one-time “StrikeLab Full” purchase unlocks unlimited analyses and every feature. It is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account, so it can be restored on new devices. We never receive your card or payment details.

To check and restore the purchase, the app connects to the Google Play Store app on your device. The number of free analyses you have used is stored on your device only. The Google Play Billing library may send Google basic diagnostic information (such as device model, Android version and app version) to keep the service working.

06 / YOUR CHOICE

What you choose to share

When you share a shot card, a highlight video, an export of your sessions or error details, StrikeLab hands it to the app you pick in Android’s share menu (for example email or a messaging app). Nothing is shared unless you do this, and what happens next is governed by that app’s privacy policy.

07 / BACKUP

Android backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may back up StrikeLab’s data to your own Google account, as it does for other apps. This backup is controlled by you in your device settings, and we have no access to it.

08 / ADVERTISING

Advertising

StrikeLab shows no ads and does not use your advertising ID.

09 / AGE & PRIVACY

Children

StrikeLab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10 / THIRD PARTIES

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except as described above (Google Play for the purchase, Google ML Kit diagnostics).

11 / DATA CONTROL

Data retention and deletion

  • We hold no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • To delete individual shots or sessions, delete them in the app. To delete everything, uninstall StrikeLab or clear its data in Android settings. Highlight videos saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them.
  • For data held by Google, use your Google Account controls: myaccount.google.com
12 / USER RIGHTS

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es ). Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests concern Google’s services and are best made through Google. You can always contact us with questions.

13 / UPDATES

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
