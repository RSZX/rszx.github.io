TraceDrive – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

TraceDrive is a game developed by Triquinete (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is collected when you play TraceDrive on Android or Windows, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address or any account.
  • Your settings, best times and medals stay on your device.
  • The free version of TraceDrive on Android shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent first.
  • The optional “Full Game” purchase unlocks every circuit and removes all ads. It is processed by Google Play, and we never see your payment details.
  • The Windows version has no ads and no purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

TraceDrive saves the following locally on your device, in the app’s own private storage:

  • settings you choose (graphics, effects, sound volume, vibration, fullscreen on PC);
  • your best lap times and medal progress;
  • whether you own the full game, and a count of races finished (used to space out ads).

This data is never sent to us. You can reset best times from within the app’s settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows full-screen ads at natural breaks only: when you leave the results screen after finishing a race, at most once every few races. Ads never appear while you plan your line or race.

To provide these ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and clicks;
  • diagnostic data.

Depending on your consent and location, this information may be used to show personalised ads, or only non-personalised (limited) ads.

Google’s handling of this data is described in:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Once you buy the full game, TraceDrive no longer loads ads or the advertising SDK.

Your consent choices (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, TraceDrive shows a consent message (Google’s User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it. You can:

  • accept personalised advertising;
  • reject it, in which case you may still see non-personalised (limited) ads;
  • manage your choices by purpose and partner.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the game. The game works the same whatever you choose. Declining never limits access to any part of the game.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).

US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with consumer privacy laws (such as California), you can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising through the privacy message shown in the app, or in Settings → Privacy options.

In-app purchase (“Full Game”)

The optional one-time “Full Game” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account, so your purchase can be restored on new devices. We never receive your card or payment details.

Permissions

The Android app uses these permissions:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to contact Google Play for purchases;
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, as described above;
  • Billing: to make and restore the in-app purchase through Google Play;
  • Vibrate: for the vibration setting.

TraceDrive does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or files.

Children

TraceDrive is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except as described above (Google AdMob for ads, Google Play for purchases).

Data retention and deletion

  • We hold no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • To delete your local game data, uninstall TraceDrive or clear its data in Android settings.
  • For data held by Google, use your Google Account controls: myaccount.google.com

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es). Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests concern Google’s services and are best made through Google. You can always contact us with questions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
