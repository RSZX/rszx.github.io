TraceDrive – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

TraceDrive is a game developed by Triquinete (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles when you play TraceDrive on Android or Windows, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address or any account.
  • TraceDrive does not connect to the internet, shows no ads and has no purchases.
  • Your settings and best times stay on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information. TraceDrive does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

TraceDrive saves the following locally on your device, in the app’s own private storage:

  • settings you choose (graphics, effects, sound volume, vibration, fullscreen on PC);
  • your best lap times and medal progress.

This data never leaves your device. You can reset best times from within the app’s settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Permissions

The Android app requests only the VIBRATE permission, which powers the vibration setting. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or files.

Children

TraceDrive is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new “Last updated” date.
