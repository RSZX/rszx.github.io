OpenGrounds – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

OpenGrounds is a game developed by Triquinete (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is collected when you play OpenGrounds on Android, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address or any account.
  • Your saved games and settings stay on your device.
  • OpenGrounds has no ads and no analytics.
  • The optional “Full game” purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

OpenGrounds saves your settings (graphics, sound, controls), your saved games and autosave, and whether the full game is unlocked in files on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

In-app purchase (“Full game”)

The optional one-time “Full game” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account, so your purchase can be restored on new devices. We never receive your card or payment details.

To check and restore the purchase, the game connects to the Google Play Store app on your device. The Google Play Billing library may send Google basic diagnostic information (such as device model, Android version and app version) to keep the service working. Google’s handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

OpenGrounds shows no ads and does not use your advertising ID.

Children

OpenGrounds is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except as described above (Google Play for purchases).

Data retention and deletion

  • We hold no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • To delete your local game data, uninstall OpenGrounds or clear its data in Android settings.
  • For data held by Google, use your Google Account controls: myaccount.google.com

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es). Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests concern Google’s services and are best made through Google. You can always contact us with questions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
