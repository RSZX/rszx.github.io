Become a tester.

OpenGrounds is getting ready for Google Play. Before it can be published, Google requires the game to go through a closed test: a group of testers has to keep it installed for 14 consecutive days. Join the list and help get it launched.

Application OpenGrounds
Platform Android
Test length 14 days
Cost Free
01 / HOW IT WORKS

How it works

  1. Sign up with the email of your Google Play account.
  2. We add you to the closed test and send you the opt-in link.
  3. Accept the invite and install OpenGrounds from Google Play.
  4. Keep the game installed for 14 days. Playing and sending feedback is very welcome.
02 / YOUR EMAIL

What we do with your email

Your email is only used to add you to the OpenGrounds test on Google Play and to contact you about the test. It is never shared or used for anything else. See the OpenGrounds privacy policy.
